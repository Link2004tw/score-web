# User Guide

A quick guide for teachers using the Scoreboard app.

---

## Getting Started

### Sign Up

1. Open the app in your browser
2. Click **Sign Up**
3. Enter your **display name**, **email**, and **password** (minimum 6 characters)
4. Click **Sign Up**

You'll be redirected to the Leaderboard.

### Login

On future visits, click **Login** and enter your email and password.

---

## Leaderboard

The leaderboard displays all students ranked by score (highest first).

### Search & Filter

- **Search by name** — Type in the search box to filter students by name
- **Filter by gender** — Use the Gender dropdown to show only Male or Female students
- **Filter by grade** — Use the Grade dropdown to show students in a specific grade

Filters can be combined (e.g., female students in "3 primary" whose name starts with "A").

### Viewing a Student

Click any student's name to view their full details, including when they were added.

---

## Managing Students

### Add a Student

1. Click **Add Student** in the navigation bar
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Name** — Student's full name
   - **Grade** — Select from the dropdown (KG1 through 6th Primary)
   - **Gender** — Male or Female
   - **Score** — Starting score (default 0)
3. Click **Add Student**

You'll be redirected to the student's detail page.

### Edit a Student

1. Navigate to the student's detail page (click their name on the leaderboard)
2. Click **Edit**
3. Update any fields
4. Click **Save Changes**

### Delete a Student

1. Navigate to the student's detail page
2. Click **Delete**
3. Confirm the deletion (this action cannot be undone)

---

## Adjusting Scores

The Score page is the main tool for quickly updating scores.

### Set the Adjustment Amount

At the top of the Score page, you'll see an **Amount** field. Set this to whatever value you want to add or subtract (default is 1).

### Adjust a Student's Score

Each student has two buttons:

- **−** (minus) — Decreases the score by the amount shown
- **+** (plus) — Increases the score by the amount shown

When you click a button:

- The score updates immediately
- You'll see a **pop animation** on the score number
- The score turns **primary colour** (increase) or **red** (decrease) during the animation
- Scores cannot go below 0

### Searching on the Score Page

Use the search bar to quickly find a student without scrolling through the full list.

### Example

To give 3 students 15 points each:

1. Set the Amount to **15**
2. Find the first student and click **+**
3. Find the second student and click **+**
4. Find the third student and click **+**

Each click updates immediately with visual feedback.

## Mobile Tips

- The app is fully responsive and works on phones and tablets
- On small screens, the navigation links remain accessible at the top
- The score buttons are large enough to tap with a finger
- Filters stack vertically on narrow screens for easy one-handed use

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can multiple teachers use the app?

Yes. Each teacher signs up with their own account. However, all teachers share the same student database — there are no per-teacher data silos in the current version.

### Is there a maximum score?

No. Scores can be any non-negative number. There is no upper limit.

### Can I import students from a spreadsheet?

Not yet. Students must be added one at a time through the Add Student form.

### What happens if I delete a student by accident?

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Use with caution.
